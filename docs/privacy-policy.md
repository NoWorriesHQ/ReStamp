# ReStamp privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

ReStamp puts the date taken and GPS location back into photos and videos that lost them when they were edited. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

- Your photos and videos stay on your phone. ReStamp never uploads them anywhere.
- There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The only things that leave your phone are map pictures and place searches, which go to OpenStreetMap when you use the map. Purchases go through Google Play.

## Your photos and videos

ReStamp reads and changes only the files and folders you pick. It reads their date taken and location tags, and writes new ones when you ask it to. All of this happens on your phone.

To read the real location in your photos, ReStamp asks for Android's **media location** permission. Without it, Android hides the GPS in photos from apps.

Before ReStamp changes a file, it saves a copy of the part it's about to change, so you can undo the change from **Recent changes**. These copies stay in the app's private storage on your phone. They're deleted after 30 days, and also when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

## The map and place search

When you set a location by hand, ReStamp shows a map from OpenStreetMap and lets you search for a place:

- **Map pictures** come from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org). Your phone asks for the part of the map you're looking at.
- **Place search** sends the text you type, such as "Port Vila", to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). It's sent only when you press search.

Like any website, these services see your phone's internet address and the request. ReStamp sends nothing else: no photos, no file names, and not your own location. The OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy covers what they do with requests: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

## Purchases

Bulk copy is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and ReStamp only learns whether you own it. Google's privacy policy covers your payment information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Media location | To read the real GPS in your original photos and videos |
| Internet and network state | For the map, place search and Google Play purchases |
| Google Play billing | To sell the bulk copy unlock |

ReStamp doesn't ask for your device's own location. It doesn't ask for your contacts, camera or microphone either.

## Children

ReStamp isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.
